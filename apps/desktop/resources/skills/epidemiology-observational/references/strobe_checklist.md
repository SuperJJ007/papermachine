# STROBE items mapped to outputs

| # | Item | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Study design | Named design; time zero |
| 6 | Participants | Eligibility, sources, follow-up methods; matching criteria for case-control |
| 7 | Variables | Exposure, outcome, confounders, effect modifiers — with definitions |
| 9 | Bias | Efforts to address sources of bias (DAG, new-user design) |
| 10 | Study size | How arrived at |
| 12 | Statistical methods | Confounder control, interactions, missing data, loss to follow-up, sensitivity analyses |
| 13 | Participants | Flow numbers; reasons for non-participation |
| 14 | Descriptive data | Characteristics table; follow-up time (total & average) |
| 15 | Outcome data | Numbers of events or summary measures over time |
| 16 | Main results | Unadjusted and adjusted estimates with CI; which confounders and why; absolute risk for meaningful period |
| 17 | Other analyses | Subgroups, interactions (additive + multiplicative), sensitivity, E-value |
| 19 | Limitations | Direction and magnitude of potential bias |
| 20 | Interpretation | Cautious, non-causal language unless justified |
