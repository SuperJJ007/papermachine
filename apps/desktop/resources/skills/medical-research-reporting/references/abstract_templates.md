# Structured abstract templates

## RCT (JAMA style, ≤350 words)
**Importance** one sentence on the gap. **Objective** To determine whether X vs Y improves Z in [population]. **Design, Setting, and Participants** [Design] conducted at [n] centers in [country] from [date] to [date]; [n] adults with [condition]; final follow-up [date]. **Interventions** [n] randomized to X; [n] to Y. **Main Outcomes and Measures** Primary: [outcome] at [time]. Secondary: [list]. **Results** Among [N] randomized (mean age, [x] years; [n] [%] women), [n] ([%]) completed the trial. The primary outcome occurred in [n/N (%)] vs [n/N (%)] (adjusted [measure], [est] [95% CI, L–U]; P = .xx). [Key secondary]. [Serious AEs] occurred in [%] vs [%]. **Conclusions and Relevance** Among [population], X compared with Y [did/did not] result in [effect]. **Trial Registration** [registry] Identifier: [ID].

## Cohort study (STROBE, ≤300 words)
**Background** · **Methods** (design, data source, period, exposure, outcome, confounders, model) · **Results** (n, person-years, events, crude and adjusted HR/RR with CI, absolute risks, key sensitivity) · **Conclusions** (association language, implication).

## Meta-analysis (PRISMA)
**Background** · **Methods** (databases, dates, eligibility, RoB tool, model, heterogeneity, registration) · **Results** (k studies, N participants; pooled effect with CI and PI; I²; subgroup/bias findings; GRADE) · **Conclusions**.

## Diagnostic accuracy (STARD)
**Objective** · **Design/Setting/Participants** (consecutive? blinded?) · **Index test & reference standard** · **Results** (n, prevalence, Se/Sp/LR± with CI, AUC with CI, indeterminates) · **Conclusions**.

## Prediction model (TRIPOD+AI 2024)
**Objective** (develop/validate; outcome; horizon) · **Methods** (data, predictors, model, internal validation, sample size) · **Results** (events, AUC apparent/corrected, calibration slope, decision curve range) · **Conclusions** (intended use; need for external validation).
