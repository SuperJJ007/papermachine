# Condensed checklists (item numbers follow the official documents)

These are navigational summaries. The reporting-checklist tool stores a **working map of official item IDs**. It is self-reported coverage, not independent verification and not a substitute for the publisher PDF.

## CONSORT 2025 (30 items) — default for new randomised trials

Hopewell et al. *BMJ* 2025;388:e081123. Open science (2–5) is new; item 8 is patient/public involvement; item 21b defines analysis populations; item 30 is limitations (generalisability is no longer a standalone item).

1 Title/abstract · 2 Trial registration · 3 Protocol and SAP · 4 Data/code sharing · 5 Funding and conflicts of interest · 6 Background · 7 Objectives (benefits and harms) · 8 Patient and public involvement · 9 Trial design · 10 Changes after start · 11 Setting · 12 Eligibility (participants; sites/deliverers) · 13 Intervention and comparator · 14 Outcomes · 15 Harms assessment methods · 16 Sample size · 17 Sequence generation · 18 Allocation concealment · 19 Implementation · 20 Blinding · 21 Statistical methods, analysis population, missing data, additional analyses · 22 Participant flow · 23 Recruitment dates · 24 Intervention as administered · 25 Baseline table · 26 Outcomes and estimation · 27 Harms results · 28 Ancillary analyses · 29 Interpretation · 30 Limitations

Historical **CONSORT 2010** (25 items) remains as guideline `CONSORT_2010` for manuscripts that already used that edition.
